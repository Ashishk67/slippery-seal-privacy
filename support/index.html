How to play

  • Steer: swipe left or right.
  • Jump: swipe up.
  • Drop fast: swipe down while you are in the air.
  • Barrel roll: one long, quick swipe left or right.
  • Poop at the orca: on water sections a POOP button slides in at the bottom right. A shot only lands while the orca is directly behind you, and the arrow above the orca lights up when you are lined up.
  • Underwater: swipe up to rise and down to dive. Watch the breath meter and surface for air before it empties.

The same list is in the game: tap the gear at the top right of the title screen, then How to play.

Levels and Endless

PLAY starts the next level you have not finished. LEVELS opens the full list, where each level shows the stars you have earned. Endless mode unlocks once you finish level 5 — that is the mode with no finish line, where the game keeps getting faster until something catches you.

Playing with friends

Tap PLAY WITH FRIENDS on the title screen. One player taps CREATE A ROOM and shares the four-letter code; everyone else types that code and taps JOIN. Up to four seals race the same track.

  • Everyone needs the app installed and an internet connection.
  • There is no matchmaking and there are no public rooms. You can only race people you have given the code to.
  • There is no chat, and you cannot send another player anything you have typed. The name above a rival seal is chosen by the game.
  • If a race will not start, check that everyone is on the same version of the app.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login, and the whole game is available from the first launch. On Android you may optionally sign in to Google Play Games if you want your score on the global leaderboard.

Is there anything to buy?
No. This version has no in-app purchases and no advertising. Fish are earned by playing and are spent only on colours, hats and other cosmetic items. Nothing you unlock makes you faster or harder to catch: there is no pay-to-win, and there never will be.

The game runs slowly or stutters.
Open the gear on the title screen and set Graphics to Medium or Low. The game also steps this down by itself if it detects a device that is struggling.

There is no sound.
Check Music and Sound effects in the same Settings card. On iPhone the game plays through the silent switch, so that is not the cause.

I lost my progress.
Progress is saved on your own device only. It is not tied to an account and does not transfer between devices, and uninstalling the app removes it.
