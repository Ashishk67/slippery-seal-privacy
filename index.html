Privacy Policy for Slippery Seal

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Slippery Seal ("the app", package com.slipperysealgame.slipperyseal) is developed by ASHISH KUMAR ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no network access of any kind.

Information we collect

None. The app requests no Android permissions and contains no code that sends information anywhere. We have no servers and receive nothing from your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress — high score, in-game fish currency, owned and equipped cosmetic items, missions, and settings — to a single file in the app's own private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporting, or social media integrations. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Test builds

Builds distributed through Google Play testing tracks are subject to the same policy. Note that Google Play itself may collect information about installations and crashes independently of the app; that processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
