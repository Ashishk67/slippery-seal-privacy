Delete your data — Slippery Seal

App: Slippery Seal · Developer: ASHISH KUMAR · Last updated: 24 August 2026

Slippery Seal has no user accounts and no servers. We do not operate a database of players and we hold no personal data about you. The only information that ever leaves your device is your leaderboard score and your Google Play Games display name, and those are held by Google, not by us. The steps below are how you delete them.

1. Delete your leaderboard scores and gamer profile

These are stored by Google Play Games Services. To remove them:

  1. Open play.google.com/games/profile, or the Google Play Games app on your phone.
  2. Open the settings for your Play Games profile.
  3. Find the option to delete your Play Games data. Google lets you delete the data for a single game or your whole Play Games profile — choose whichever you want.
  4. If you are deleting for one game only, pick Slippery Seal from your list of games.

Google occasionally changes the wording of these settings. If you cannot find the option, the same data can be reviewed and deleted from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com.

2. Delete the data stored on your device

Your high score, in-game fish currency, cosmetic items, missions, settings and your device's own best-run list are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Slippery Seal deletes all of it immediately. You can also use Android Settings → Apps → Slippery Seal → Storage → Clear data to reset it without uninstalling.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhere it livesOn deletion
Leaderboard scores Google Play Games Services Deleted. No additional retention period by us, because we never hold a copy.
Play Games display name and player ID Your Google Account Deleted with your Play Games profile, under Google's retention policy.
Game progress, currency, cosmetics, settings Your device only Deleted immediately when you uninstall or clear app data.
Any data held by the developer None — we have no servers Nothing to delete.

Need help?

If you cannot complete these steps, email slipperysealgame@gmail.com and we will help you find the right setting. Note that because we hold no data ourselves, we cannot delete it on your behalf — it has to be done through your own Google Account.
