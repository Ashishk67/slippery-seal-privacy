Privacy Policy for Slippery Seal

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Slippery Seal ("the app", package com.slipperysealgame.slipperyseal) is developed by ASHISH KUMAR ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

We do not run any servers and we do not receive your personal data. The app has no advertising and no analytics.

The only feature that uses the network is the optional global leaderboard, and it exists only in the Android version, where it is provided by Google Play Games Services. The iPhone and iPad version makes no network connections at all and collects nothing.

Which version you are using

  • iPhone / iPad (App Store, TestFlight): the app has no leaderboard, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no network access of any kind. Nothing leaves your device. Only the "Information stored on your device" section below applies to you.
  • Android (Google Play): everything below applies, including the optional leaderboard.

The leaderboard (Android only)

On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the app can submit your run score to a leaderboard operated by Google, and can display the leaderboard to you. In that case:

  • Your score and your Play Games display name and player ID are processed by Google in order to rank and show the leaderboard.
  • That information is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not store it ourselves, and we have no database of players.
  • Other players may see your Play Games display name and score on the leaderboard. This is the normal behaviour of a public leaderboard.
  • The app never asks you to type a name. There is no nickname field and no chat, so you cannot enter personal information into the app.

Opting out

The leaderboard is optional. If you are not signed in to Google Play Games, or you decline the sign-in prompt, or you are offline, the app shows your own best runs stored on your device instead, and nothing is transmitted. You can sign out or manage your Play Games profile and data at any time in the Google Play Games app.

Deleting your data

On iPhone and iPad there is nothing to delete anywhere but your own device: deleting the app removes everything it ever stored.

Full step-by-step instructions are on a dedicated page: Delete your data. In short: your leaderboard scores and Play Games display name are deleted through your own Google Account, and everything else is on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. We hold no copy of any of it.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress — high score, in-game fish currency, owned and equipped cosmetic items, missions, settings, and your device's own best-run list — to files in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social media integrations. The only third-party service is Google Play Games Services, described above. We do not sell data to anyone.

Children

The app is directed to a general audience aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Google Play Games Services applies its own restrictions to accounts belonging to minors.

Test builds

Builds distributed through Google Play testing tracks and through Apple TestFlight are subject to this same policy. Google Play itself may collect information about installations and crashes independently of the app; that processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
