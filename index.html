Privacy Policy for Slippery Seal

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Slippery Seal ("the app", package com.slipperysealgame.slipperyseal) is developed by ASHISH KUMAR ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

We do not collect or store your personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

The app uses the network for two optional features:

  • Race mode ("Play with friends"), on both iPhone/iPad and Android. When you race, the app connects to a small relay server we run so that the players in your room can see each other. The server keeps nothing once the room closes.
  • The global leaderboard, on Android only, provided by Google Play Games Services.

If you use neither, the app does not connect to anything.

Which version you are using

  • iPhone / iPad (App Store, TestFlight): there is no leaderboard. The only network feature is race mode, described below.
  • Android (Google Play): everything below applies, including race mode and the optional leaderboard.

Race mode

Race mode is private and invite-only. One player creates a room and shares a short room code, and up to four players who have that code can join. There are no public lobbies and no matchmaking with strangers.

While you are in a room, the app sends the following to our relay server, which passes it on to the other players in the same room:

  • a race name chosen by the game for your seal;
  • the colour and accessories your seal is wearing;
  • during a race, your seal's position, speed and actions (such as jumping and pushing);
  • your finish time and a few race counters (such as pushes landed and barrel rolls) used for the end-of-race awards.

How this is handled:

  • Only the players in your room see it. They see your race name, how your seal looks, and how you raced.
  • It is not stored. The server holds it in memory only while the room exists. It is gone as soon as the host leaves, everyone leaves, or the room has been idle for 10 minutes. It is not written to disk, not logged, and not kept in any database.
  • The name you type for your seal stays on your device. It is not sent to other players or to our server.
  • There is no chat. You cannot send messages, text, pictures or audio to other players.
  • The connection is encrypted in transit.
  • The relay server is hosted by Fly.io (currently in Singapore). As with any internet connection, the server and Fly.io see your device's IP address while you are connected, so that data can reach you. Our server does not record it. Fly.io's handling of connection data is covered by the Fly.io Privacy Statement.

The leaderboard (Android only)

On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the app can submit your run score to a leaderboard operated by Google, and can display the leaderboard to you. In that case:

  • Your score and your Play Games display name and player ID are processed by Google in order to rank and show the leaderboard.
  • That information is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not store it ourselves, and we have no database of players.
  • Other players may see your Play Games display name and score on the leaderboard. This is the normal behaviour of a public leaderboard.
  • The leaderboard never shows the name you give your seal.

Opting out

Both network features are optional, and the rest of the game works fully offline.

  • Race mode: if you never open "Play with friends", the app never connects to our relay server.
  • Leaderboard (Android): if you are not signed in to Google Play Games, or you decline the sign-in prompt, or you are offline, the app shows your own best runs stored on your device instead, and nothing is transmitted. You can sign out or manage your Play Games profile and data at any time in the Google Play Games app.

Deleting your data

Race mode leaves nothing to delete: our server does not keep anything once a room closes.

On iPhone and iPad, everything else is on your own device, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Full step-by-step instructions are on a dedicated page: Delete your data. In short: your leaderboard scores and Play Games display name are deleted through your own Google Account, and everything else is on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. We hold no copy of any of it.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (high score, in-game fish currency, owned and equipped cosmetic items, missions, settings, the name you give your seal, and your device's own best-run list) to files in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social media integrations. The third-party services involved are:

  • Google Play Games Services (Android only), for the leaderboard, described above.
  • Fly.io, which hosts the race-mode relay server on our behalf, described above.

We do not sell data to anyone.

Children

The app is directed to a general audience aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Google Play Games Services applies its own restrictions to accounts belonging to minors.

Test builds

Builds distributed through Google Play testing tracks and through Apple TestFlight are subject to this same policy. Google Play itself may collect information about installations and crashes independently of the app; that processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Some test builds released before this update showed the name you typed for your seal to the other players in your race room, instead of a name chosen by the game. That name was handled in the same way as the rest of the race data above: passed only to the players in the room, held in memory only while the room existed, and never stored or logged.
