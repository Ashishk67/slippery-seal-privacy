Delete your data — Slippery Seal

App: Slippery Seal · Developer: ASHISH KUMAR · Last updated: 13 September 2026

Slippery Seal has no user accounts, and we do not store your data on any server. We do not operate a database of players and we hold no personal data about you. Race mode uses a relay server we run, but it only passes race information between the players in a room and keeps nothing once the room closes. On Android, your leaderboard score and Google Play Games display name are held by Google, not by us. The steps below cover everything that can be deleted.

1. Delete your leaderboard scores and gamer profile (Android only)

These are stored by Google Play Games Services. To remove them:

  1. Open play.google.com/games/profile, or the Google Play Games app on your phone.
  2. Open the settings for your Play Games profile.
  3. Find the option to delete your Play Games data. Google lets you delete the data for a single game or your whole Play Games profile — choose whichever you want.
  4. If you are deleting for one game only, pick Slippery Seal from your list of games.

Google occasionally changes the wording of these settings. If you cannot find the option, the same data can be reviewed and deleted from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com.

2. Delete the data stored on your device

Your high score, in-game fish currency, cosmetic items, missions, settings, the name you gave your seal and your device's own best-run list are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling Slippery Seal deletes all of it immediately.

  • Android: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Apps → Slippery Seal → Storage → Clear data to reset it without uninstalling.
  • iPhone / iPad: touch and hold the app icon, then choose Remove App → Delete App.

3. Race mode: nothing to delete

When you race with friends, your seal's race name, its colour and accessories, its position and actions during the race, and your finish time are passed through our relay server to the other players in your room. The server holds this in memory only while the room exists, and deletes it automatically when the host leaves, everyone leaves, or the room has been idle for 10 minutes. It is never written to disk, logged or stored, so there is nothing left for anyone to delete.

Need help?

If you cannot complete these steps, email slipperysealgame@gmail.com and we will help you find the right setting. Note that because we hold no data ourselves, we cannot delete it on your behalf — leaderboard data has to be deleted through your own Google Account, and everything else through your device.
