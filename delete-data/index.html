Delete your data — Slippery Seal

Developer: ASHISH KUMAR · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Slippery Seal does not require a developer-created account and does not operate a persistent database of player accounts or cloud game saves. Information is handled in several places, so deleting the app does not erase every category of information.

1. Delete local progress and settings

This removes the app's local progress, golden fish, cosmetics, scores, seal name, settings and locally saved reward or trial state.

  • Android: open Settings → Apps → Slippery Seal → Storage, then choose Clear storage or Clear data. You can also uninstall the app. Wording varies by device.
  • iPhone: choose Remove App → Delete App. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data and is not equivalent to deleting it.

Any separate device or cloud backup must be managed through the relevant platform's backup settings. Clearing local data does not delete store purchase records or Google Play Games data.

2. Delete Google Play Games data

On Android, leaderboard and profile information is managed through Google Play Games.

  1. Visit your Play Games profile.
  2. Open Your Data, then Delete Play Games Account & Data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Slippery Seal and choose Delete.

You can alternatively use the Play Games profile settings in Google Play. For the current steps, see Google's deletion instructions.

Deleting one game's data does not delete your entire Google Account. Google Play History is a separate setting and may need to be managed separately. Playing again while signed in may create new game data.

3. Advertising information and privacy choices

Where available, open Slippery Seal's Settings → Ads & purchases → Ad privacy choices to review or change advertising choices.

On Android, you can also find advertising-ID controls in your device's privacy or Ads settings. Available controls and their names vary by Android version and device.

Changing consent, resetting or deleting an advertising identifier, or uninstalling the app does not automatically erase previously processed advertising information.

For information held by Google, use the privacy controls and request options described in Google's Privacy Policy. Google may retain certain information for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or other purposes described there.

4. Purchase information

Apple or Google maintains records of purchases made through its store. Deleting Slippery Seal does not erase these records, cancel a completed purchase or automatically issue a refund.

The automatic-ad removal purchase can be checked or restored using the relevant store account. The game provides Settings → Ads & purchases → Restore purchases. It is not a subscription.

For store-held information, refunds and account privacy requests, use Apple's or Google's account and support tools. We do not receive your payment card number or operate a separate purchase database.

5. Friend-race information

Our relay processes temporary room and race information in memory. It does not maintain a persistent player or race database. Rooms close when the host leaves or all players leave; inactive rooms are set to expire after ten minutes.

Our relay does not write race payloads or player IP addresses to application logs. Hosting infrastructure information is handled by Fly.io under the Fly.io Privacy Statement.

6. Support messages and privacy requests

If you have emailed us, we hold the correspondence and attachments you provided. To request access, correction or deletion, email slipperysealgame@gmail.com and describe the information or previous conversation concerned.

We will handle requests under applicable law and may need to verify that you are entitled to make the request. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or an unresolved dispute. We will explain applicable limits rather than promise deletion of information controlled by another provider.

Please do not send passwords, full payment details or identity documents unless a specific, necessary verification process has been explained.
